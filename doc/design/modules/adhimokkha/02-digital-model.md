# Adhimokkha (အဓိမောက္ခ) — digital model

**State:** researched design specification; this file does not certify implementation. Read [Myanmar explanation](01-buddhist-explanation.md) before this document and [implementation design](03-implementation.md) afterward. Actual delivery evidence belongs to [status](../../../delivery/status.md).

## Purpose and interpretation

Represent explicit provisional resolution: whether available support permits choosing a direction, requires further evidence or remains unresolved. It is not a fact-certifier, numeric certainty score or replacement for intention and authorization.

The source identifiers AD-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### AD-01 — Supported resolution

A proposed choice names the supporting basis, material uncertainty and relevant criterion; model confidence cannot be its only basis.

### AD-02 — Revisability

A choice remains revisable when premises, evidence or controls change. Preserve the prior resolution and supersession link.

### AD-03 — Explicit unresolved state

Insufficient support yields unresolved or needs-evidence rather than a forced answer. Simple supported routines do not require elaborate uncertainty prose.

### AD-04 — No authority transfer

Resolution cannot expand scope or mark an external effect complete. Cetana coordinates intention and sikkha independently authorizes.

### AD-05 — Action-relative adequacy

Enough support to ask a question is different from enough support for destructive input or claiming a task complete.

## Cooperation and ownership

Adhimokkha represents provisional resolution; panna assesses support; cetana coordinates intention; sikkha constrains. These associated facets are not a literal serial account of mental factors.
