# Vaca (ဝါစာ) — digital model

**State:** researched design specification; this file does not certify implementation. Read [Myanmar explanation](01-buddhist-explanation.md) before this document and [implementation design](03-implementation.md) afterward. Actual delivery evidence belongs to [status](../../../delivery/status.md).

## Purpose and interpretation

Vaca owns communication interpretation routines and truthful, attributable expression of adopted outcomes. It supports focused conversation, quoted replies, actual questions and proactive reports without turning every message into an adviser request.

The source identifiers VA-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### VA-01 — Sufficient routine

Handle genuine simple messages locally; route complex contextual interpretation through configured cognition only when needed. Do not confuse keyword matching with complete understanding.

### VA-02 — Grounded expression

Answer from valid supplied evidence, distinguish uncertainty and reported advice, and never claim unread content or unexecuted actions.

### VA-03 — Focused questions

When operator input is necessary, publish the actual question in the relevant conversation; private reflection must not masquerade as a delivered question.

### VA-04 — Reply continuity

Quoted replies retain the exact source message/goal where applicable. Conversation changes do not reset the individual concern.

### VA-05 — Paced contact

Proactive speech and milestone reports pass Quiet, delivery and contact checks. Queued contact remains undelivered until actually published.

## Cooperation and ownership

Vaca communicates; sanna interprets meaning; cetana coordinates intention; kamma executes external work; infrastructure transports provider calls; vithi/contact policy serializes publication. A model fluent sentence is not an observed outcome.
