# Nivarana (နီဝရဏ) — digital model

**State:** researched design specification; this file does not certify implementation. Read [Myanmar explanation](01-buddhist-explanation.md) before this document and [implementation design](03-implementation.md) afterward. Actual delivery evidence belongs to [status](../../../delivery/status.md).

## Purpose and interpretation

Introduce an evidence-grounded review of unhelpful software patterns: unsupported repetition, silent topic hopping, defensive rejection of corrections or unproductive indecision. These are engineering observations, never diagnoses of actual hindrances, moral character or mental illness.

The source identifiers NI-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### NI-01 — Observable pattern basis

Require actual decision/outcome sequence references and the failed concern relation before recommending a change. No diagnosis from a label, latency or one uncertain answer.

### NI-02 — Distinguish healthy uncertainty

Missing evidence, denied capability and legitimate clarification are not faults to suppress. Preserve honest unresolved states.

### NI-03 — Bounded correction

Recommend a changed evidence step, reconsidered premise or explicit defer/release; do not punish the agent or manufacture distress.

### NI-04 — No autonomous authority

Pattern review cannot alter permissions, identity, budgets or core policy. Controls and actual effect uncertainty remain independent.

### NI-05 — No forced positivity

Beneficial behavior includes acknowledging mistakes and unpleasing truths. Pleasant tone does not establish absence of unhelpful behavior.

## Cooperation and ownership

Nivarana is a research and pattern-review boundary, not another mind. Paccavekkhana supplies actual history, viriya considers effort, panna inspects support and sikkha retains independent constraints.
