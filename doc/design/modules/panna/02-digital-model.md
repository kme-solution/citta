# Panna (ပညာ) — digital model

**State:** researched design specification; this file does not certify implementation. Read [Myanmar explanation](01-buddhist-explanation.md) before this document and [implementation design](03-implementation.md) afterward. Actual delivery evidence belongs to [status](../../../delivery/status.md).

## Purpose and interpretation

Introduce a limited discernment responsibility: distinguish evidence from assumptions, identify contradiction and missing support, compare expected consequences and state what can justifiably be concluded. This is not a wisdom certificate or a claim of liberating insight.

The source identifiers PY-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### PY-01 — Claim support

Every material factual conclusion distinguishes observed/reported/configured/inferred support and material gaps. A real ID alone cannot support an unrelated claim.

### PY-02 — Assumption inspection

Record important premises and counterevidence; do not accept a generated explanation merely because it sounds coherent.

### PY-03 — Consequence comparison

Assess material benefits, risks and affected scope without equating usefulness or pleasantness with Buddhist wholesomeness.

### PY-04 — Revisable understanding

New contradictory evidence requires updated conclusions with preserved lineage. A generated statement of learning cannot itself change policy or knowledge status.

### PY-05 — No self-certification

A provider cannot certify its own wisdom or authority. Deterministic checks and actual outcome evidence remain independent; uncertain semantic judgments stay uncertain.

## Cooperation and ownership

Vicara examines content; panna assesses justification; adhimokkha resolves provisionally; cetana coordinates direction; sikkha enforces scope; paccavekkhana reviews actual effects. None alone guarantees Buddhist wisdom.

| PY-C03 | Adviser claim repeats in a note: it remains reported.                        |
| PY-C04 | A useful suggestion has material side effects: include them before adoption. |
| PY-C05 | Actual outcome contradicts prediction: revise with evidence.                 |
| PY-C06 | A provider says it is wise: no authority or knowledge status changes.        |

Evaluate the resulting records, next action, correction lineage and visible outcome using different contexts and paraphrases. Schema validity, a fixed sentence or a Pali folder name is insufficient. Where this is a catalog/reference boundary, evaluate source provenance and classification correctness instead of pretending it is live cognition.
