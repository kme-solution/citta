# Chanda (ဆန္ဒ) — digital model

**State:** researched design specification; this file does not certify implementation. Read [Myanmar explanation](01-buddhist-explanation.md) before this document and [implementation design](03-implementation.md) afterward. Actual delivery evidence belongs to [status](../../../delivery/status.md).

## Purpose and interpretation

Chanda supplies attributable interests, desires and candidate concerns for one continuing agent, including free curiosity when nobody is speaking. A motive can propose engagement; it neither creates an observed fact nor grants execution rights.

The source identifiers CH-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### CH-01 — Attributable motive

Distinguish operator-configured interests, remembered concerns and self-proposed interests. Preserve origin, scope and uncertainty; never invent a biography to justify a topic.

### CH-02 — Persistent concern

Retain selected inquiry, source, last outcome, next step and deliberate detour/return condition across conversations.

### CH-03 — Free initiative

Allow a fresh interest or imagined question without pending work or immediate environmental relevance. Keep conjecture inferred and use real sources for factual claims.

### CH-04 — Release and revision

A bounded inquiry can settle, block or defer; explain why. Completion of a desire does not silently complete another task or require deleting the lasting interest.

### CH-05 — Control and pacing

Continuous internal eligibility is not a doctrine-derived clock or compulsory filler. Respect Pause/Stop, actual per-goal/provider budgets and Quiet contact policy.

## Cooperation and ownership

Chanda proposes engagement; vithi schedules; manasikara orients; cetana coordinates intention; sati supplies memory; paccavekkhana supplies discrepancies. A task ledger is not itself chanda.
