## Purpose and interpretation

Introduce an effort policy deciding whether a bounded inquiry has a justified next step, should change method, needs input or should release that inquiry. This is distinct from resource budgets and must preserve the user requirement for independent continuing initiative.

The source identifiers VR-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### VR-01 — Effort basis

Base continuation on an available next evidence step, revised interpretation or unresolved question, not elapsed time alone.

### VR-02 — Method adjustment

After repeated no-progress output, propose a different permitted evidence step or an honest limitation; do not merely regenerate the same reflection.

### VR-03 — Resource distinction

Budgets constrain execution independently. Zero hourly limit means uncapped accounting mode, not proof all work is useful.

### VR-04 — Bounded release

Release or defer a particular inquiry with its source/return condition; do not silently pause the entire continuing mind or impose a new arbitrary idle cutoff.

### VR-05 — Controls and recovery

Pause/Stop, unknown effects and unavailable permissions override proposed effort. A new interest cannot bypass an exhausted or uncertain source task.
