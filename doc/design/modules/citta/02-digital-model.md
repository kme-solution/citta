# Citta / vinnana (စိတ် / ဝိညာဏ်) — digital model

**State:** researched design specification; this file does not certify implementation. Read [Myanmar explanation](01-buddhist-explanation.md) before this document and [implementation design](03-implementation.md) afterward. Actual delivery evidence belongs to [status](../../../delivery/status.md).

## Purpose and interpretation

Introduce citta as a shared, versioned consideration contract assembling one current object and its associated modeled facets. It is not another consciousness engine, a persistent inner self or a replacement for the serialized vithi owner. The name is an engineering analogy, not a claim of experience.

The source identifiers CI-S1 onward belong to document 01. The requirements below are original engineering choices informed by that research, not statements attributed to the Buddha. Source-layer and terminology limits in document 01 apply. No database field, model response or software timing establishes a literal mental factor.

## Behavioral requirements

### CI-01 — One consideration identity

Assign one authoritative consideration/decision identity and object/source version envelope. Every included facet refers to that envelope rather than independently selecting an object.

### CI-02 — Optional honest facets

Routine answers need only relevant supported facets. Missing recognition, appraisal or deliberation detail remains absent; do not fabricate universal mental factors to fill a row.

### CI-03 — Candidate versus adoption

Separate an assembled candidate from an adopted decision, dispatched operation and actual feedback. Provider return alone cannot adopt state.

### CI-04 — Interruption continuity

An interrupt creates a new consideration while preserving the displaced concern/return step. Do not mutate a past consideration to make it seem uninterrupted.

### CI-05 — No second owner

The assembly is pure; vithi retains serialized adoption, PostgreSQL remains domain truth and controls apply across all facets.

## Cooperation and ownership

Citta assembles associated software facets; manasikara orients, cetana coordinates intention and vithi serializes/adopts. Do not equate their function-call order with doctrinal co-arising or infer consciousness from a coherent record.
